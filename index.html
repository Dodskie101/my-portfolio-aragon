Who am I?
My Identity.
My name is Mark Lawrence M. Aragon, I am 20 years old, I currently live in Zone-3, Kalabaylabay, El Salvador City, Misamis Oriental, I was born on January 2 2006. My hobbies are playing online games such as Chess, Mobile Legend, Magic Chess Gogo and many more. I also love to listen to music, code, play the strings and learn new things. My motto in life is "The more I suffer, the stronger and better I become".
Why Did I Choose BSIT?
The History.
Way back when I was just a child I was totally fascinated by technology and I made a promise to myself that when I grow up I will learn more about it. Despite poverty, those obstacles did not stop me — they became an inspiration. I strived every day learning and finding ways to access technology. After graduating Senior High School I decided to continue this journey and chose Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, knowing that pursuing this will make me happy as it aligns with my passion since the beginning.
